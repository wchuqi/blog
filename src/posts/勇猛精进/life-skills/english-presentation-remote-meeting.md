## 场景

内部产品大会远程汇报，现场上百人，由主持人开场后交接给汇报人（产品经理 Jason），介绍与另一团队协作完成的产品集成项目。

## 开场与致谢环节

主持人交接：

> And I'm gonna kick it off to Jason first to give us an overview.

接棒开场（自报家门 + 致谢）：

> Thank you so much for the intro, and thank you all for joining us here today. My name is Jason and I am a product manager.

> We're going to show you a really nice and cool integration we've been collaborating on.

关键表达：

- **give us an overview** — 做个整体介绍（主持人常用的交接话术）
- **a quick shout out to everyone who's been involved in this project** — 向参与项目的每个人致敬/致谢
- **Not all heroes wear capes** — 英雄不问出处（致谢团队的俏皮说法）
- **an incredible display of cross-functional teamwork across two different teams** — 跨团队协作的精彩示范
- **I really appreciate everyone's dedication and collaboration** — 感谢大家的投入与协作

## 背景铺垫（Level Set）

换页话术：**Next slide, please.**

正式介绍前先对齐认知：

> Before we get started, I wanted to do a quick level set to make sure especially for those of you who might not be familiar with it...

用类比解释产品：

- The simplest way to think about it is a lead intake form — 它本质上是一个线索收集表单
- It serves as the digital front door for potential clients — 它是潜在客户的"数字化前台"
- The primary goal is to help clients get connected and get a quick estimate — 核心目标是帮助客户建立连接并快速获得估价

产品的四个核心动作：

1. **Building the client's profile** — 构建客户画像，弄清客户是谁、来做什么
2. **Dynamically surface product recommendations and tailor messaging based on their input** — 根据客户输入动态推荐产品、定制文案，确保 value proposition 契合客户需求
3. **Route the leads** — 分发线索，通过省略重复问题加速流程，让客户更快建立连接（因为大多数客户已被竞品预先筛选过，要为双方节省时间）
4. **Optimized for performance marketing traffic** — 针对营销流量优化：自然流量、付费流量、联盟流量各有不同的落地页和文案

## 业务问题（为什么要做这个集成）

1. **Limited chat coverage（在线客服覆盖不足）** — 客户量大但客服不够，导致错失机会（missed opportunities）
2. **Disconnected systems（系统割裂）** — 一个产品采集的数据不一定能流转到另一个产品，生态内体验不连贯；prefill 等跨产品数据流没有做到 airtight
3. **由此引出的客户问题**：客户被要求重复填写已提供过的信息。类比：

> I'm pretty sure everyone here can resonate: when you're visiting a medical facility, you're asked to fill in information online before visiting, but by the time you get there, you're asked to fill the same form again.

## 解决假设（Hypothesis）

> If we're able to identify the client intent early, meet them where they are by providing options that best suit their needs, and streamline handoff between the different systems, we are able to create a more cohesive and efficient experience.

这将帮助提升满意度（satisfaction）和申请转化率（application conversion）。

关键表达：

- **meet them where they are** — 在客户所处的地方迎接他们（贴合客户当前状态提供服务）
- **streamline handoff between systems** — 打通系统间的交接流程
- **top of funnel / middle to bottom of funnel clients** — 漏斗顶部/中下部的客户
- **ensure a smooth experience when passing the client from one product to another** — 保证客户在产品间流转的顺畅体验

## 收尾点评（领导反馈）

> What makes me most excited is just the teams working together, learning from each other.

> Not overthinking it — get going, get started, make it better. It's not going to be perfect day one, but it's a start, and you get there and you learn and keep going.

要点：

- 不必等完美再动手：先启动、再改进（get going → make it better）
- 团队互补的数据模型优势：能感知用户意图（user intent），把已知的用户信息带到下一个触点
- **cook up some pretty fun things** — 一起搞出有趣的东西（展望合作的口语表达）
